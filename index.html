What counts as pressure

Every ball gets a win probability before and after it. A pressure ball is one of the tightest fifth in its innings, where the result can swing most. Two extras passed a test set in advance: knockout balls for batters and balls straight after a 30-run, three-over burst for bowlers, counted only when they also sit in the tighter half of the innings.

Is it real?

On pressure balls a wicket falls 6.1% of the time against 5.4% on the rest. The benchmark never sees the player's name and was tested only on matches it wasn't trained on.

Limits

A record, not a forecast. Qualifying needs 200 pressure balls with 50 in the IPL: 52 batters and 66 bowlers. Price, fitness and XI fit are out of scope.
